5-MINUTE RENDER KEEP-ALIVE

1. Deploy the bot on Render.
2. Open UptimeRobot and create an HTTP(s) monitor.
3. URL:
   https://YOUR-SERVICE.onrender.com/health
4. Choose the Free plan / 5-minute interval.
5. Save the monitor.

Do NOT add a self-ping timer inside the bot for this purpose: an internal timer cannot keep a sleeping Render instance alive. The request needs to come from outside Render.
